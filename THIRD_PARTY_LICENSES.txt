1. FFmpeg 7.1.1 (Minimal Static LGPL Build)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Project / Source: https://ffmpeg.org/
Source Archive:   https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-7.1.1.tar.xz
Build Script:     scripts/build_min_ffmpeg_msys2.sh (MSYS2 MINGW64)
License:          GNU Lesser General Public License v2.1 or later (LGPL v2.1+)
Full License Text: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html

This distribution embeds a minimal static LGPL build of FFmpeg 7.1.1 compiled
with `--enable-schannel` (Windows native TLS) and `--enable-w32threads`
(Windows native threads), without `--enable-gpl` and without `--enable-nonfree`.
No third-party GPL codecs or MSYS2 runtime DLLs are linked.

Configure Flags (see scripts/build_min_ffmpeg_msys2.sh):
  --target-os=mingw32 --arch=x86_64 --enable-static --disable-shared
  --enable-small --disable-debug --disable-doc --disable-htmlpages
  --disable-manpages --disable-podpages --disable-txtpages
  --disable-ffplay --disable-ffprobe --disable-avdevice --disable-postproc
  --disable-autodetect --disable-pthreads --enable-w32threads
  --disable-everything --enable-schannel
  --enable-decoder=aac,aac_latm,h264
  --enable-protocol=file,pipe,http,https,httpproxy,tls,tcp,crypto,data
  --enable-demuxer=hls,mpegts,mov,matroska,concat,aac,mp3,ogg,flv
  --enable-muxer=mp4,ipod,matroska,webm,mpegts,null
  --enable-parser=h264,hevc,av1,vp9,vp8,aac,aac_latm,opus,vorbis,mpegaudio
  --enable-bsf=aac_adtstoasc,h264_mp4toannexb,hevc_mp4toannexb,extract_extradata,vp9_superframe,av1_metadata
  --extra-cflags="-Os -std=gnu17 -ffunction-sections -fdata-sections"
  --extra-ldflags="-static -static-libgcc -Wl,--gc-sections -s"

Runtime Override Notice (LGPL Relinking / Replacement):
Users may substitute their own `ffmpeg.exe` at runtime by placing it in the
same directory as `AniDownloader.exe` or in a `bin\ffmpeg.exe` subdirectory
beside `AniDownloader.exe`, which takes precedence over the embedded binary.
